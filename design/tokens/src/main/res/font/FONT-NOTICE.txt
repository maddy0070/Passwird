Why these are bundled rather than fetched
-----------------------------------------
A webfont request on launch would tell a CDN each time the user opens their password
manager. Bundling costs about 950 KB in the APK and leaks nothing.

Why Plex rather than Inter or Roboto
------------------------------------
Partly character: Roboto reads as stock Android, which this product's design direction
explicitly rules out, and Inter is ubiquitous enough to be invisible.

Mostly, though, it is functional. Plex Mono disambiguates the glyph pairs that matter when
someone is reading a generated password aloud or typing one into a console: 0 against O,
and 1 against l against I. For the component that renders secrets, that is a correctness
requirement, not a stylistic preference.
