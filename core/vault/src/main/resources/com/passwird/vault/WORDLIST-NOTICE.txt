wordlist-eff-large.txt
======================

Source:  EFF's Long Wordlist for random passphrases
         https://www.eff.org/dice
         https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt

Author:  Joseph Bonneau, Electronic Frontier Foundation (2016)
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Modification: the original file is tab-separated "<dice roll>\t<word>". This copy
contains only the word column, one word per line, in the original order. No words
were added, removed or altered, so the list remains exactly 7776 entries and each
word therefore contributes log2(7776) = 12.9248 bits of entropy.

Why this list: it was designed specifically for human-memorable random passphrases —
words are 3-9 characters, unambiguous when read aloud, free of profanity and of
easily-confused near-duplicates, and prefix-distinct enough to be typed reliably.
Those properties are what make a passphrase people will actually keep.
